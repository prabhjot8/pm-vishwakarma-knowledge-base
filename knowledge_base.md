# Knowledge Base: PM Vishwakarma Scheme

## 1. Purpose
The PM Vishwakarma scheme supports traditional artisans and craftspeople working with their hands and tools. This file organises scheme knowledge into categories that can be searched, updated, and reused.

## 2. Eligibility — summary
- The applicant should be engaged in one of the traditional trades covered by the scheme.
- The applicant should be at least 18 years old on the date of registration.
- The applicant should be actively engaged in the relevant trade at registration.
- Scheme benefits are generally limited to one member per family, subject to the official definition and rules.
- Government employees and their family members are excluded under the scheme conditions.
- Prior participation in specified credit schemes may affect eligibility; exceptions and time periods must be checked against the current official guidelines.

## 3. Support and benefits — summary
The scheme's support framework has included:
- Recognition through a PM Vishwakarma certificate and identity card.
- Skill assessment and basic training, with advanced training as an optional pathway.
- A training stipend, subject to applicable requirements.
- A toolkit incentive, subject to scheme conditions.
- Collateral-free enterprise credit in tranches, subject to eligibility and repayment conditions.
- Concessional borrower interest terms and government interest subvention.
- Incentives for eligible digital transactions.
- Marketing support, which may include quality certification, branding, e-commerce onboarding, and trade-fair access.

**Do not treat the summary above as a guarantee of payment or approval.** Benefit amounts, delivery methods, and qualifying conditions must be verified in the latest official scheme documents.

## 4. Registration and verification — summary
The scheme has provided online enrolment through designated Common Service Centres (CSCs) and the official portal/app. The documented process includes local-level verification followed by district and state-level review/approval stages.

Applicants may be asked for identity, contact, bank-account, and family information. The exact documents and authentication steps should be confirmed through the official process.

## 5. Knowledge representation
A useful representation separates:
- **Entities:** Applicant, Family, Traditional Trade, Training, Toolkit Incentive, Loan Tranche, Digital Transaction, Marketing Support, CSC, Verification Committee.
- **Attributes:** age, trade, training status, loan repayment status, application status.
- **Relationships:** APPLIES_FOR, PRACTISES, BELONGS_TO_FAMILY, COMPLETES, RECEIVES, VERIFIED_BY, REPAYS, QUALIFIES_FOR.
- **Rules:** age requirement, trade coverage, family limit, exclusion checks, and prerequisites for loan tranches.

## 6. Example knowledge statements
- An applicant practises a covered traditional trade.
- An applicant submits a scheme registration through an approved channel.
- An application is reviewed by the prescribed verification authorities.
- Basic training is a prerequisite for the first loan tranche under the stated scheme conditions.
- A second loan tranche depends on repayment and additional qualifying conditions.

## 7. Limitations
This repository is a structured educational summary. It does not contain a complete legal interpretation of every scheme clause. Verify every operational decision against the latest official guidelines and official portal before acting.
