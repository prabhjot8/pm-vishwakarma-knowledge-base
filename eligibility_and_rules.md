# Eligibility and Rules

This file records the main checks to consider when reviewing a hypothetical application. It is a learning aid, not an automated official eligibility decision.

## Eligibility checks

| Rule ID | Check | Evidence to verify | Outcome if not satisfied |
|---|---|---|---|
| EL-01 | Applicant is at least 18 on registration date | Valid age/identity evidence as accepted by the official process | Flag for review; do not mark eligible |
| EL-02 | Applicant practises a covered traditional trade | Trade details and verification under current guidelines | Flag for review |
| EL-03 | Applicant is actively engaged in the trade at registration | Local/official verification | Flag for review |
| EL-04 | Family-member limit is respected | Family details and existing benefit records, as applicable | Escalate for eligibility review |
| EL-05 | Government-employment exclusion is checked | Applicant and family status, as required by the rules | May be ineligible |
| EL-06 | Prior credit-scheme restrictions and exceptions are checked | Prior loan/scheme history and dates | Review against current official conditions |

## Benefit and process conditions

| Rule ID | Condition | Verification needed |
|---|---|---|
| BN-01 | Certificate and ID are issued only through the prescribed process | Application approval/status |
| BN-02 | Training support is provided under the scheme's training process | Assessment, attendance, and training completion records |
| BN-03 | Toolkit incentive is subject to the prescribed eligibility and process | Skill assessment and scheme record |
| LN-01 | First loan tranche depends on basic training and applicable credit conditions | Training completion, bank assessment, and current scheme rules |
| LN-02 | Second loan tranche requires repayment and other qualifying conditions | First-tranche repayment, account status, elapsed time, and current criteria |
| DG-01 | Digital transaction incentive applies only to eligible transactions | Transaction records and monthly scheme limits |
| MK-01 | Marketing support is subject to programme availability and relevant criteria | Beneficiary status and support-specific requirements |

## Decision procedure (high-level)
1. Confirm the current version of the official guidelines.
2. Check age and covered-trade requirements.
3. Check active engagement and family restrictions.
4. Check exclusions and prior credit-scheme history.
5. Check registration and verification status.
6. For each benefit, independently verify its specific prerequisites.
7. Record the source, date checked, and reason for any unresolved decision.

## Important
Do not use this table as a substitute for the official application/verification system. Some rules have exceptions, and exact time limits or amounts may change.
